# CareerShift Development Phases

**Project:** CareerShift – AI Powered Career Intelligence Platform  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Development Roadmap

# Overview

CareerShift will be developed in multiple phases to ensure scalability, maintainability, and production readiness.

Each phase builds upon the previous one, allowing continuous testing, validation, and incremental feature delivery.

# Success Criteria

CareerShift will be considered production-ready when:

- All frontend modules are integrated with backend APIs.
- PostgreSQL schema is fully implemented.
- AI Intelligence Engine generates accurate career insights.
- Assessment workflow is fully functional.
- Reports can be generated and downloaded.
- Workshops support enrollment and progress tracking.
- Admin portal provides complete platform management.
- Security, testing, monitoring, and deployment meet production standards.

---

# Final Vision

CareerShift aims to become an AI-first Career Intelligence Platform that empowers professionals to embrace AI, continuously improve their skills, and make informed career decisions through personalized assessments, actionable insights, learning roadmaps, and guided workshops.
